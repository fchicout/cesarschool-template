\chapter{Future Work}
%don't forget to define a unique label to use with the \ref command
\label{chap:trabalhos_futuros}

We've reached the part where you admit, with class and a slight touch of embarrassment, that you didn't have time to do everything you wanted to... This section is basically the "I promise I'll deliver properly next time, professor."

Future work is that list of tasks the researcher leaves for someone in the future (usually themselves, once they start their master's degree lol). This is where you toss out grandiose ideas that, in the end, boil down to "It would be cool to do this, but there wasn't time."

You can also dream big: "In the future, we intend to integrate this system with AI based on deep learning, IoT, and blockchain." All of this coming from a project that barely runs on your 8-year-old laptop with 4GB of RAM.

The important thing here is to show that you have vision, ambition... and that you know your work is just the first step on a long road, possibly full of improvements suggested by the committee.

In short, this section is where you try to plant a little seed of doubt in your evaluators' minds: "Did he not do it because it was hard, or because it was a strategic choice to leave it for the future?" Spoiler: it was because it was hard.
