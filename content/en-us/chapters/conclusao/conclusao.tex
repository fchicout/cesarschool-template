\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusao}

We've finally reached the glorious moment of the conclusion! Also known as the part where you try to tie everything together as if you'd planned it that way from the very beginning, and definitely didn't use ChatGPT to write an initial draft.

In this section, you look back, take a deep breath, and write grand sentences like: "The results obtained partially corroborate the initial hypotheses..." Translation: "It sort of worked, and we're pretending that was the plan all along."

The conclusion is where you reaffirm that, despite the limitations, the missing data, and the code that only runs if you restart your computer three times and say a little prayer, your work does contribute to the field - even if only minimally. Maybe even as a cautionary tale of what not to do...

And of course, don't forget the classic closing line: "It is hoped that this work will serve as a foundation for future research." Which is the academic equivalent of saying: "Thanks, and good luck to whoever comes next."

That is precisely what I, the author of this template's fork, wish for the students who now use it for their own work. I hope this template serves as a foundation for future work, helping to ease some of the formatting burden. Cheers, and good luck to you!
