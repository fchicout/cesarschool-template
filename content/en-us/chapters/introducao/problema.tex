\section{Research Problem}
\label{problema}

If the reader has made it this far, your introduction convinced them to keep reading. Congratulations! Anyway, every thesis needs a research problem - something that bothers you, a gap in knowledge... that thing you noticed nobody wanted to solve... probably because it's a lot of work.

You show up and say: \enquote{This is wrong, and I, a mere student with a \$0.00 budget, am going to try to fix it while also trying to survive the semester and get my degree!}

Using phrases like \enquote{Despite advances in the field, it is observed that...} is a great way to disguise the fact that you discovered this problem while doing an assignment for a different class and thought: \enquote{Hmm, this could be a thesis...}
