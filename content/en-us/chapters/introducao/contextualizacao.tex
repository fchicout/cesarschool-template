\chapter{INTRODUCTION}
\label{chap:contextualizacao}

Every thesis begins with an Introduction, that section where you try to convince the reader your topic is relevant and indispensable to humanity - even if it is really just a system that sends a notification when the pizza comes out of the oven.

The introduction is also where you tell an exciting story: from the problem nobody had solved, to your solution (which occurred to you at 3am, while you were tossing and turning trying to fall asleep).

You describe the context, present the challenge, add a bit of drama, and finish with a question like: \enquote{Given this, how can we guarantee the efficiency of the proposed methodology in real-world scenarios?} You have no idea. Your work is precisely about finding the answer.
