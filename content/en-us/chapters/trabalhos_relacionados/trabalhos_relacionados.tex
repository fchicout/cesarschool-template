\chapter{Related Work}
\label{chap:trabalhosrelacionados}

Related work is that moment where you say: \enquote{See, dear committee, other people already tried, failed, succeeded, and inspired me (or made me go down a completely different path).}

In this part of the thesis, you present the other works, point out strengths and areas for improvement in each one, and by the end make it clear that your work is the protagonist of this whole series.

This is also where you show that nobody has solved your exact problem (thankfully), but plenty of people came close - close enough for you to say: \enquote{Unlike previous studies, this work proposes an approach that...} Translation: I took their idea and gave it a light tune-up.

You will cite each of the related works, for example: one of the related works was \cite{akhtar2018threat}. Scientific works are generally preferred here, but to cite an online source, you can use \citeonline{apple2024faceid}.
