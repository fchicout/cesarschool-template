\chapter{Threats to Validity}
%don't forget to define a unique label to use with the \ref command
\label{chap:ameacas_validade}

Imagine your thesis is a castle. A castle built out of 12pt Arial, pie charts (yes, pun very much intended given the topic), tables, and that beautiful paragraph you copied... I mean, drew inspiration from, from some foreign paper. But like any construction, cracks eventually start to show. Those cracks are your threats to validity - basically, everything that could make your work look less trustworthy. It's that moment when someone shows up and says: "Nice experiment... but did you test this on three people and a cat?"

So, getting ahead of these threats is a great way to show that you've already considered these possibilities and aren't oblivious to the risks. Part of this text can also serve as input for the future work section.
